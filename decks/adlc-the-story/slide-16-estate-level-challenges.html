Four Challenges One Layer Up

The Fragmentation

Every team picks AI tools independently; multiplied across teams, the estate stops having one shape. Needs the Technical/Integration layer wired, not just one project's harness.

The Governance Gap

Security policy predates agentic AI, silent on what it can read, generate, or send. Owned at the Business Context layer, above any single project.

The Attribution Gap

Nobody can tell finance which teams get real value from AI spend. Needs session-level usage dashboards grouped by scoped phase, not one undifferentiated total.

Vendor Lock-In

Whatever a team runs today won't be what they run in 18 months. A portable, tool-agnostic context foundation means an adapter swap, not a rebuild.
