The Mental Model Shift

Developer mindset (before)

Primary artifact

Code is the artifact. The reviewer reads the diff and ships it.

Source of truth

"I know the codebase" lives in my head, not in anything the AI can read.

Success metric

Judged on lines shipped. Skipping docs just means moving faster.

Engineer mindset (after)

Primary artifact

Context is the artifact. Review the HLD; the diff follows from it.

Source of truth

"I know the codebase" lives in docs the AI can also read.

Success metric

Judged on direction set. Skipping docs breaks the next AI session, not just the next hire.

The owners  Engineer defines the goal and makes architectural calls  |  AI follows the workflow and implements incrementally  |  Both review every phase boundary

Code used to be the artifact. Now the context is: a redefinition of what "doing the job well" means, not a new IDE shortcut.
