## Try your first task

For a completed walkthrough, tell the user: “You can run a skill by asking for an outcome right here. Paste your material or share a link to a source we verified.” Suggest one first task and say why it fits. If the intended source is blocked, give the specific recovery action and a prompt that works with pasted material.

Show these three examples in chat, leading with the suggested skill. Adapt the wording to the user's project when known.

| Skill                 | What to ask                                                                                                            | What to expect                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Meeting notes         | “Turn these workshop notes into decisions, actions and open questions.” Then paste the notes.                          | A private draft with action owners, deadlines and gaps. You can request edits before approving an exact version for Confluence. |
| Requirements analysis | “Review this brief against our launch objectives and flag what needs clarification.” Include the brief and objectives. | A requirements matrix with source references, assumptions, gaps and questions for stakeholders.                                 |
| Story generation      | “Turn these reviewed requirements into user stories with testable acceptance criteria.” Include the requirements.      | Candidate stories mapped to the requirements, with dependencies and open scope questions for backlog review.                    |
