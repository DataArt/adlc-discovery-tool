## Why This Matters

This engagement doesn't get to assume a blank slate. If [[CLIENT_NAME]] already has an internal initiative, the solution this engagement produces — Pilot, Scale, Evolve — is wired to it, not built alongside it as a competing track:

- Build on existing AI infrastructure and tooling where it already exists, rather than standing up parallel infrastructure.
- Respect existing ownership and governance — decisions already made by the internal initiative aren't relitigated here without cause.
- Where this engagement's recommendation conflicts with the initiative's direction, that conflict is surfaced explicitly, not quietly overridden.

## Wiring & Respect

- **Ownership boundary** — what stays with the internal initiative's owner, and what this engagement can actually influence
- **Integration point** — where the pilot plugs into existing tooling, governance, or reporting, rather than creating a parallel track
- **Escalation path** — who to loop in if the pilot's recommendation and the initiative's roadmap diverge
- **Coordination cadence** — how often this engagement checks back with the initiative's owner while it runs
