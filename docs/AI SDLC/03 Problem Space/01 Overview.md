## How This Diagnosis Is Organised

Two pages under [Diagnose](./02%20Diagnose/02%20Problems.md) are the reader-facing digest — a few minutes to a full picture, no context of the engagement required:

| Page                                                         | Answers                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Where Gaps Sit](./02%20Diagnose/01%20Where%20Gaps%20Sit.md) | What's the path from idea to production, and where do diagnosed Problems cluster — time or otherwise? |
| [Problems](./02%20Diagnose/02%20Problems.md)                 | What's broken, how sure are we, and where's the evidence?                                             |

A third page, [Claims](./02%20Diagnose/03%20Claims.md), isn't part of that two-minute digest — it's the append-only ledger of quotes each Problem draws on, one row per claim. Diagnose extracts these, not Capture: drafting or updating a Problem means tracing its cited SDLC Domain evidence back to the specific Workshop Notes it came from and pulling the supporting quotes into the ledger. It's what makes a Problem's move from 🟡 Reported to 🟢 Confirmed checkable: a second claim from a different workshop pointing at the same Problem ID, not just a stronger sentence.

What's already been tried toward AI-assisted delivery, and what's already measured about delivery today, are raw current-state facts, not diagnosis — they live in [Capture — Current State: AI Adoption](/AI%20SDLC/02%20Capture/01%20Current%20State/AI%20Adoption.md) and [Capture — Current State: Metrics](/AI%20SDLC/02%20Capture/01%20Current%20State/Metrics.md). Problems cites both where a named problem draws on them.

Everything in [SDLC Domains](/AI%20SDLC/02%20Capture/00%20Overview.md#sdlc-domains) is the evidentiary layer behind the two Diagnose pages — ten SDLC domains, each holding the raw current-state findings, time-loss estimate, AI opportunity, and evidence still needed for that domain. SDLC Domains change as workshops run; Diagnose changes when a finding is solid enough to promote into the reader-facing picture.
