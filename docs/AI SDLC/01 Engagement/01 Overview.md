## What ADLC Is

**ADLC** — DataArt's AI-first SDLC Discovery — is a structured, evidence-led Solution Design engagement: it diagnoses where an organisation's actual SDLC bottlenecks sit, not the ones assumed at kickoff, and maps a concrete path to AI-first workflow adoption from there. "Evidence-led" is load-bearing — every finding traces back to a workshop or conversation, not an assumption. See [04 Evidence Chain](./03%20Process/04%20Evidence%20Chain.md) for the mechanism that enforces it.

The engagement runs three gate-bound phases — Investigate, Solution, Roadmap — each opened and closed by a gate. The full phase/stage breakdown, and how they connect end to end, lives in [03 Process](./03%20Process/01%20Overview.md); this page stays one level up, at the level of what the engagement is and why it exists.

For the broader industry story this engagement sits inside — why SDLC bottlenecks are common across organisations, and what the underlying harness does about them once a pilot proves out — see [The Story](/The%20Story/).
