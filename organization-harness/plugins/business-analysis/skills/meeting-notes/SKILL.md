# Meeting Notes

## Operating principles

Read [operating principles](references/operating-principles.md) before executing. This skill is a starter draft, not an approved organizational policy.

## Required context

Transcript or notes; meeting purpose; participant names when known.

## Workflow

1. Identify the purpose and separate facts from proposals.
2. Extract decisions, actions, owners and due dates; mark missing values as TBD.
3. Capture unresolved questions and dependencies; preserve source anchors where available.
4. Present the draft for human review before any external publication.

## Expected output

Meeting summary, decisions, action table, open questions.

## Quality gate

Do not invent commitments, owners, dates or decisions.
