# Working with Business Analysis

| Skill                                                         | When to use it                                                                                                                                    | Example request                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| [Onboarding](../SKILL.md)                                     | Check workflow readiness, connect a source, resume setup, configure specialist agents, preview or schedule a recurring job, or choose a workflow. | “Resume setup for our discovery project.”                                                  |
| [Meeting notes](../../meeting-notes/SKILL.md)                 | Draft decisions and actions from meeting evidence, review job drafts and publish approved notes to Confluence.                                    | “Summarize these workshop notes and flag actions that still need an owner.”                |
| [Requirements analysis](../../requirements-analysis/SKILL.md) | Review requirements against stakeholder objectives and produce a requirements matrix, gaps and clarification questions.                           | “Review this brief against our launch objectives. Which requirements need clarification?”  |
| [Story generation](../../story-generation/SKILL.md)           | Turn a requirements baseline into candidate stories with testable acceptance criteria and traceability.                                           | “Draft stories for these approved checkout requirements and flag unresolved dependencies.” |

A team can start with meeting notes, use the decisions and open questions to refine requirements, then turn reviewed requirements into story drafts. Users can also start at any step by supplying the inputs directly. A missing connector blocks retrieval from that source. Pasted notes, briefs or requirements can still support the corresponding drafting task. Ask for the source and desired outcome, then follow the selected skill's instructions.
