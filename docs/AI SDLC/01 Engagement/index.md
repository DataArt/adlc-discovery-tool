# Engagement

Investigate · Solution · Roadmap — the three gate-bound phases that structure every ADLC engagement, from first discovery through to pilot delivery. See [01 Overview](./01%20Overview.md) for what this engagement is and why [[CLIENT_NAME]] is running one, and [02 Terminology](./02%20Terminology.md) for the vocabulary the rest of this section uses.

## How This Directory Works

[01 Overview](./01%20Overview.md) and [02 Terminology](./02%20Terminology.md) orient the whole engagement — what it is, why it exists, and the words it uses — and don't change during the engagement. [03 Process](./03%20Process/01%20Overview.md) holds the Phase/Stage/Artifact process definition itself, inspired by [DataArt's Connect Framework](./06%20Reference/01%20Connect%20Framework.md). The pages around it each cover one concern of _how_ the engagement runs; each stage's actual content lives in its own dedicated section — [04 Goals](/AI%20SDLC/04%20Goals/), [03 Problem Space](/AI%20SDLC/03%20Problem%20Space/), [05 Solution Space](/AI%20SDLC/05%20Solution%20Space/), [06 Roadmap](/AI%20SDLC/06%20Roadmap/) — not here.
