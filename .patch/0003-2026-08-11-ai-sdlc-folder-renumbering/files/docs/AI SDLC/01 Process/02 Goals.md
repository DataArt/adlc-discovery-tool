## Common Goals

Goals seen recurring across engineering estates with legacy systems and early-stage AI adoption. Use these as a prompt in the goal-setting session, not a checklist to accept as-is.

- **AI-legible codebase** — capture architectural context, integration patterns, and conventions in a form AI tools (and new engineers) can actually read and act on.
- **Estate-wide coherence** — converge on shared tools, patterns, and standards for AI-assisted output before local divergence compounds.
- **AI-aware governance** — establish governance that covers what AI tools can read, generate, and where that data flows, scoped to the organisation's actual risk surface.
- **Attributed AI spend** — give leadership a clear view of AI cost against delivery output, by team and workload.
- **Tool-agnostic foundations** — build governance and context structures that outlive any single AI tool's lifecycle.
