Act 1: An Unwired Pipeline

Legacy, as-is

Every phase interprets what came before and adds its own view. Business says "buy tickets online." Architecture adds platform concerns. BA simplifies. PM adds a date. Nothing is wrong in isolation.

By the time it reaches Development, there is no governed hierarchy of meaning. Intent degrades at every unwired handover: the customer presses Buy and gets a spinner, not the ticket.
