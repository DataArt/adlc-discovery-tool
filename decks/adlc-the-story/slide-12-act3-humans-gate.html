Act 3: Humans Gate, AI Carries

AI-assisted: the balance

Humans own intent, judgment, and consequential decisions. AI carries and enriches governed context, and does the operational work inside approved boundaries. Gates sit where meaning, risk, or authority changes.

The handover is no longer person-to-person. It is governed context, carried by AI, gated by humans: the industry-standard position, not an opinion.
