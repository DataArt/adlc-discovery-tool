## Scale the Workload to Product Context

- Extend the pilot's workload from its original squad to the wider product it belongs to — the same pattern, not a new one, applied to more of the estate.
- Note any hard deadlines (e.g. planned team expansions) that bound how far Scale can responsibly extend before [Evolve](./05%20Evolve.md) needs to be in place.
- Keep both engineering and business/process stakeholders in the loop as scope grows — the pilot's success criteria need re-validating in business terms as the audience widens, not assumed to still hold.

## Wire Governance Control (Product → Project)

- The pilot's lightweight, workload-scoped guardrails need a decision-rights layer above them once more than one project is involved — who owns scope-change sign-off and exceptions at the product level, not just the project level.
- Data classification agreed for the pilot's scope in [Pilot](./03%20Pilot.md) gets applied consistently as more projects and teams fall under it, rather than re-derived per project. See [Problem Space — Governance](/AI%20SDLC/03%20Problem%20Space/03%20SDLC%20Domains/09%20Governance).

## Set Up a Wider Knowledge Base

- Extend the pilot's scoped knowledge base to the rest of the product, following the same phased pattern — ingest, curate, validate — rather than a big-bang rollout.
- Build on whatever ingestion or integration work is already declared as [[CLIENT_NAME]]'s own next step, rather than inventing a new rollout path. See [Problem Space — Knowledge Base](/AI%20SDLC/03%20Problem%20Space/03%20SDLC%20Domains/04%20Knowledge%20Base).

## Control

- Access and permissions reviewed as the user base grows past the pilot's original small group — not assumed to still be appropriate at product scale.
- Usage stays visible per team and workload as more people onboard, so the widening scope doesn't outrun what's being watched.

## Open Questions

- Who owns the decision to extend a pilot from one squad to the wider product — Engineering alone, or a joint call with Product?
- What's the smallest set of user groups to onboard first, and in what order?
- Who signs off on governance exceptions once decision-rights move from project to product level?
