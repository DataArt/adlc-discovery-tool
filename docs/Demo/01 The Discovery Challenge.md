# The discovery challenge

[Presentation index](index.md) · [Next](<02 Tool Capabilities.md>)

**Agenda section 1 · Suggested slide 2**

## Audience takeaway

Discovery needs to preserve context across handoffs so the team can explain what to improve and why.

## Where meaning gets lost

![Existing repository illustration showing business intent being reinterpreted through delivery handoffs](../public/images/context/From%20Business%20Ideas%20to%20Customer%20Confusion.png)

**Caption:** Each person can make a reasonable interpretation while the original intent becomes harder to recover.

**Talk through the picture:** Start at the business request, point to the accumulating interpretations, and end at the customer experience. This is the repository’s illustrative story, not a Collinson incident.

## What better continuity requires

![Existing repository illustration of humans reviewing consequential decisions while AI carries context through the lifecycle](../public/images/context/AI-Powered%20Software%20Lifecycle%20Pipeline.png)

**Caption:** People provide intent and judgment; reviewed context carries that understanding forward.

**Presenter boundary:** This illustration depicts the broader target lifecycle. ADLC Discovery investigates the situation and designs the path toward it; the discovery tool does not itself execute every illustrated delivery activity.

## Speaker script

### Presenter narrative

> Today I want to introduce the ADLC Discovery Tool: what it is, how we use it to run discovery, and how different people can contribute. We’ll also hear directly from two colleagues about their experience using it. At the end, I’ll show a short example from a real engagement.
>
> The starting problem is familiar: understanding is spread across conversations, meeting notes, tickets, and documents. Different people hold different parts of the picture. When work changes hands, the next person often needs to reconstruct why a decision was made.
>
> When we add the question “Where could AI help?”, we need to understand the existing workflow, the actual problems, and the outcomes the organization wants. Otherwise, it is difficult to explain why a particular intervention is worth trying.
>
> ADLC Discovery gives the team a shared structure for gathering that context, reviewing it, and developing a solution and delivery plan.

**Audience prompt:** “Where do you most often have to reconstruct context in your work?”

Take one or two responses. Keep this focused on the general challenge; save the detailed client story for section 6.

### Transition

> Let me show you what the tool brings together and how its workspace is organized.
