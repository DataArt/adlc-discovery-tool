## Audience takeaway

Participants should leave knowing what they can contribute and the agreed next step for trying the tool.

## Facilitation script

### Discussion

Start with audience questions. Use these prompts if needed:

- Which part of the tool would be most useful in your role?
- What remains unclear about contributing or reviewing work?
- What support would you need to get started?
- Where could we apply this approach next?

Invite both colleagues to answer questions about their own experience. Capture detailed technical setup questions for follow-up when necessary.

### Next steps and closing

> Let’s agree on the next useful step: who wants to explore the tool, which engagement could use it, and what support is needed.

| Action                                                     | Owner   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Share tool access and contribution guidance                | [OWNER] |
| Identify a candidate engagement or onboarding session      | [OWNER] |
| Follow up on unanswered questions and contributor feedback | [OWNER] |

Fill in only actions the group agrees to.

> We’ve looked at how ADLC Discovery organizes the work, how people contribute, and what starting an engagement involves. Thank you to [PERSON 1] and [PERSON 2] for sharing their experience. Our agreed next step is [ACTION], with [OWNER] taking it forward.
