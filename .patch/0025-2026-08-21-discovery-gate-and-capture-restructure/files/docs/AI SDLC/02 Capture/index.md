## How This Space Works

[00 Overview](./00%20Overview.md) defines the terminology (Workshop / Session / Topic / SDLC Domain), lists the 10 SDLC Domains under diagnosis, and holds the draft workshop schedule — updated as each workshop is scheduled and completes. Each workshop below has its own folder holding an Agenda (pre-session plan), Notes (live capture), Outcomes (post-session synthesis), and a raw Transcript — shown here as full placeholder shapes for reference. In a live engagement, only Agenda exists ahead of a workshop; the other three are populated once the session actually runs.
