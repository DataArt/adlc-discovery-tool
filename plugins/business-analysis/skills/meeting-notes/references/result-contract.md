# Meeting-notes result contract

A reader who missed the meeting should be able to act on the notes and check the evidence behind a commitment. Use one draft per meeting occurrence, whether its source is a pasted transcript or a scheduled search.

| Part                 | What the reviewer receives                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Meeting and outcome  | A recognizable title, date if known, purpose, and a short summary of what changed or remains undecided.                                                                                                                                  |
| Topics discussed     | Group related discussion and decisions by topic within one set of notes, even when several projects are covered. Include relevant constraints and dependencies.                                                                          |
| Decisions            | Each settled choice and its scope, with an anchor to the agreement. Keep suggestions that still need agreement under open questions.                                                                                                     |
| Actions              | One row per commitment, with a concrete deliverable, owner, due date and source. Mark an unstated owner or deadline explicitly (TBD in general drafts, “Not specified” for the Zoom recording workflow). Preserve any stated conditions. |
| Open questions       | The unresolved choice, conflict or dependency and what would resolve it. Include a follow-up owner only when the evidence identifies one.                                                                                                |
| Sources and coverage | Identify the transcript, notes or thread used. Anchor consequential claims to a timestamp, message link, or identifiable passage in supplied notes. Flag missing material that could change the interpretation.                          |

Before returning the draft, read each decision and action against its source. Check that the wording preserves who agreed to what, the level of certainty and any conditions. Remove discussion that adds no context to an outcome. Where no decision or action is evidenced, say “None recorded in the available notes.” When discovery finds only an invitation or agenda, return “Waiting for notes” for that occurrence.

For scheduled drafts, attach the reviewer, occurrence key, revision and proposed Confluence destination to the complete title and body as specified in the [review handoff](meeting-job.md). The quality check establishes readiness for human review. Publication requires the reviewer's approval of the exact draft and destination.
