## What Goes Here

- **Business Context** — where product vision, OKRs, and stakeholder/regulatory requirements actually live today (Confluence, decks, someone's head), and who owns keeping them current
- **Operational Context** — the live sprint layer as it actually runs (Jira, Confluence, or equivalent), and how far it's trusted by the teams using it
- **Technical Context** — architecture standards and governance as they actually exist (ARB process, TDC template, company-wide conventions, if any), and whether they reach the project level
- **Integration Context** — cross-project contracts and shared event schemas: are they documented anywhere, negotiated ad hoc, or discovered by incident
- **Project Context** — per the [Landscape](/The%20Story/02%20Context%20Landscape.md) hypothesis, the layer expected to be thinnest: what actually exists per project today (README, wiki, tribal knowledge) and how it compares project to project
- Known gaps or inconsistencies already surfaced — a layer that's missing entirely, or one that exists but nobody trusts

This is raw baseline material, not synthesis. Diagnosis and root-cause analysis happen in [Problem Space](/AI%20SDLC/03%20Problem%20Space/), not here.
