- Recap and validate findings from [WS 5](../WS%205%20%28Baseline%20%26%20Guardrails%29%20-%20Date%20TBD/Outcomes.md) (Measurement · Governance) before opening new topics — confirm what was captured is accurate, surface corrections, and close any carry-forwards.
- Frame the decision this session exists to make: a recommended pilot candidate, grounded in evidence gathered across WS 1–5, not the loudest voice in the room.

## Topic 1 — Candidate Inventory

_Synthesis — draws on findings from all Problem Space Areas surfaced in WS 1–5._

> **Key question:** Which projects or products are realistic candidates for the ADLC pilot?

- Surface a shortlist of 3–6 candidate projects/products, each with basic facts: owning team, business criticality, and the current pain already evidenced in WS 1–5
- Not scoring yet — just getting the full candidate set on the table before narrowing

### Survey — Candidate Inventory

1. What projects or products are currently in flight or recently shipped that could plausibly host a pilot?
2. Who owns each candidate — which squad, and who is the accountable product/engineering lead?
3. How business-critical is each candidate — what happens if a pilot disrupts its delivery cadence?
4. Which Problem Space findings from WS 1–5 already point at this candidate as a source of time-loss?
5. Is there any candidate the room already believes is a non-starter — and why?
6. Are there candidates not yet covered by WS 1–5 evidence that still deserve consideration?

## Topic 2 — Evaluation & Scoring

_Synthesis — draws on findings from all Problem Space Areas surfaced in WS 1–5._

> **Key question:** Which candidate should the ADLC pilot start on?

- Score each candidate 1–5 per dimension, on the four evaluation dimensions already defined in [Solution Space](/AI%20SDLC/05%20Solution%20Space/) — Impact, Confidence, Ease, Governance fit — plus one pilot-readiness dimension Solution Space does not cover: Team AI-readiness
- Capture each candidate's named owner alongside the score, for context — not scored or gated, just recorded so the decision doesn't get made blind to who would actually run the pilot
- Totals (simple sum or weighted, agree live) produce a ranked shortlist
- Dimensions are a draft default, not fixed — adjust weighting or add dimensions live if the room surfaces a gap

### Survey — Evaluation & Scoring

1. **Impact** (from Solution Space) — how much of the diagnosed time-loss, per WS 1–5 Problem Space findings, would a pilot on this candidate address?
2. **Confidence** (from Solution Space) — how well-evidenced is the root cause for this specific candidate, versus anecdote?
3. **Ease** (from Solution Space) — what is the technical/data complexity and integration surface, and how fast could a squad prove the hypothesis?
4. **Governance fit** (from Solution Space) — per WS 5 Governance findings, what is the data classification and vendor/tooling fit for this candidate?
5. **Team AI-readiness** — what is the appetite, skill level, prior AI tool exposure, and psychological safety of the squad that owns this candidate?
6. **Named owner** — who is the accountable owner for this candidate, if the pilot goes ahead? (recorded, not scored)
7. Once scored, does the ranked shortlist match the room's gut instinct — if not, why not?

## Closing (~20 min)

Capture and record:

- The recommended pilot candidate and runner-up, with the scoring rationale behind the decision
- Open risks against the recommended candidate — what could stall or invalidate the pilot
- Open questions / carry-forwards, with an owner and next step for each
- This is the final workshop in the base programme — the evidence-synthesis job (pulling findings across all sessions into a prioritised pilot decision) lives here, not folded into WS 5
