# Session agenda interface

Use for a workshop or gate's `Agenda.md`. Establish the session identity, actual
or explicitly pending date, purpose, audience, duration, and the questions or
decisions the session must resolve. Link the evidence and pre-read material needed
to participate. Record preparation owners and any requested deck's location.

For workshops, map each topic to one SDLC Domain. Give it a key question, relevant
known context, and diagnostic prompts. Fit the session within two hours. After the
first workshop, include validation of prior findings and unresolved carry-forwards.
Close with what works, pain points, and open questions with owners and next actions.

For gates, identify the gate number and package being reviewed. Open with purpose,
sequence distinct decision topics with audience check questions, then finish with
an explicit ask followed by questions and next steps. State what each ask enables
and what remains blocked without it. Adapt the number and duration of blocks to
the actual package; an agenda contains proposed decisions, never approval evidence.

## Ways to express the agenda

Use a timed table, an annotated flow, or concise topic sections. Choose the format
that makes preparation and facilitation easiest; no fixed number of blocks or
mandatory diagram. A facilitator should know what to ask, which evidence to show,
and what outcome to capture without reconstructing the engagement history.
