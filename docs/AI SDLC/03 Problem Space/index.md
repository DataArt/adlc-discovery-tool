## How This Space Works

[01 Overview](./01%20Overview.md) is the status page — where the diagnosis stands, not the diagnosis itself. [02 Diagnose](./02%20Diagnose/02%20Problems.md) is the reader-facing digest: two pages, a few minutes, no engagement context required. [03 SDLC Domains](/AI%20SDLC/02%20Vision/00%20Overview.md#sdlc-domains) is the evidentiary layer behind it — one page per domain, each holding the raw current-state findings, time-loss estimate, AI opportunity, and evidence still needed, filled in as the relevant workshop session completes. Bottom-up evidence — existing initiatives, AI adoption, metrics, team pain-point data — lives in [Vision — Current State](/AI%20SDLC/02%20Vision/01%20Current%20State/Process.md) and [Vision — Inhouse Initiatives](/AI%20SDLC/02%20Vision/02%20Inhouse%20Initiatives/Overview.md), not duplicated here — both other sections cite it, neither re-hosts it.
