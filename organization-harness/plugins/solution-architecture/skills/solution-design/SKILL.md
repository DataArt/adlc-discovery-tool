# Solution Design

## Operating principles

Read [operating principles](references/operating-principles.md) before executing. This skill is a starter draft, not an approved organizational policy.

## Required context

Requirements, current system, constraints, NFRs and applicable standards.

## Workflow

1. Establish context, boundaries, constraints and gaps.
2. Explore viable alternatives and evaluate trade-offs.
3. Propose architecture, interfaces, data flows and deployment view as appropriate.
4. Record decisions, risks and unresolved assumptions; seek approval at consequential decision points.
5. Prepare the requested architecture deliverable after review.

## Expected output

Design proposal, diagrams where useful, ADR candidates, risks and traceability.

## Quality gate

Distinguish proposed from approved decisions; do not invent current-state facts.

## Optional specialist delegation

When supported and appropriate, delegate independent assessments to security-architect, cloud-architect, data-architect and integration-architect. Confirm authorization and scope first; use generic subagents with equivalent role instructions or sequential assessments if custom agents are unavailable. Consolidate evidence and conflicting findings in the main session. Do not claim parallel execution if it did not occur. Human approval remains required for consequential decisions.
