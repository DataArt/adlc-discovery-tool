The Root Cause: Context

The layer that is not kept

Business Context and Technical Context are owned and maintained by the organisation. What's routinely missing is Project Context: the live, current record of what was decided and what was built.

It is the most volatile layer in the stack, and the most valuable for AI. Without it, an AI is a navigator handed only the country map: it knows the destination, and will confidently miss the turn.
