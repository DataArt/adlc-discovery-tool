# Session outcomes interface

Use for workshop or gate `Outcomes.md`, updating it incrementally as the session's
findings are confirmed. Identify the session, summarize what it established and
did not reach, and link findings to source Notes or transcript evidence. Organize
workshop findings by SDLC Domain and gate findings by decision topic.

Keep reception separate from coverage and evidence strength. If reception is useful,
label its meaning: workshop 🟢 engaged / 🟡 neutral / 🔴 disengaged; gate 🟢 confirmed /
🟡 confirmed with caveats / 🔴 flagged. Workshop enthusiasm does not establish
priority or confirm a Problem without supporting evidence and an actual decision.

Record actions, decisions still needed, and carry-forwards with owners and dates
where known. For gates, preserve a **Gate Outcome** section containing the actual
approved or flagged result and its source. If no decision occurred, say pending.
Name flagged items, their originating stage, and the required re-evaluation.
Caveats that invalidate readiness remain open; never convert a reception dot into
gate closure automatically.

## Ways to express outcomes

A short synthesis plus a findings/action table often suffices. Add a domain map or
next-step diagram when relationships help explain the session. Avoid empty sections
and prescribed diagram styles. The reader should be able to act on each finding
and distinguish a proposal from a recorded stakeholder decision.
