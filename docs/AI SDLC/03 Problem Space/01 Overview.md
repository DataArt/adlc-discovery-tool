## How This Diagnosis Is Organised

Two pages under [Diagnose](./02%20Diagnose/) are the reader-facing digest — a few minutes to a full picture, no context of the engagement required:

| Page                                                                   | Answers                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Where Time Is Lost](./02%20Diagnose/01%20Where%20Time%20Is%20Lost.md) | What's the path from idea to production, and where does it actually stall? |
| [Problems](./02%20Diagnose/02%20Problems.md)                           | What's broken, how sure are we, and where's the evidence?                  |

What's already been tried toward AI-assisted delivery, and what's already measured about delivery today, are raw current-state facts, not diagnosis — they live in [Vision — Current State: AI Adoption](/AI%20SDLC/02%20Vision/01%20Current%20State/AI%20Adoption.md) and [Vision — Current State: Metrics](/AI%20SDLC/02%20Vision/01%20Current%20State/Metrics.md). Problems cites both where a named problem draws on them.

Everything in [SDLC Domains](./03%20SDLC%20Domains/) is the evidentiary layer behind the two Diagnose pages — ten SDLC domains, each holding the raw current-state findings, time-loss estimate, AI opportunity, and evidence still needed for that domain. SDLC Domains change as workshops run; Diagnose changes when a finding is solid enough to promote into the reader-facing picture.
