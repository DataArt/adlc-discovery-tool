# Meeting follow-up email

## Draft output

Create a reply draft to the original Outlook calendar event and keep the subject generated by Outlook. Start with a short greeting and thank-you in business English, then insert only the approved meeting notes.

Exclude **Uncertainties to clarify**, its questions, and the reviewer conversation from the Outlook draft.

```text
Hi team,

Thank you for the call. Here are the meeting notes for [Meeting title] on [Meeting date].

[Approved meeting notes]
```

Address the reply draft to everyone on the original Outlook calendar invitation. When confirmed Zoom attendance differs, list the differences in the draft-created chat notification. The BA or PM decides whether to change any recipients.

## Body formatting

Save the reply body as HTML when the Outlook action supports it. Render the approved Markdown as HTML before inserting it into the draft. Preserve section headings, bold labels, paragraph spacing, lists, tables, and clickable links. Insert the greeting and notes above any original content already present in the reply.

Verify the saved draft's formatting when the integration exposes the draft body or preview. If the Outlook action only accepts plain text, use section headings, blank lines, bullets, and readable links. Format each table row as a labeled line and tell the BA or PM in chat that the draft was saved as plain text.

## Review and send

Save the reply in Outlook Drafts for the BA or PM to review and send. Report the draft status in chat and include a direct link when available. Handle draft issues according to the [edge case rules](zoom-meeting-notes.md#edge-cases).
